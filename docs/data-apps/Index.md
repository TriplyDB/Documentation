# TriplyDB data apps/features

Data apps are features of TriplyDB, not separate products. Each app runs inside the datacloud and works on datasets you already have, so you open it from your dataset rather than installing anything new. TriplyDB currently offers data apps like saved queries, stories, editor, flows, Knowledge Graph Builder and Knowledge graph Quality test. Each has its own chapter below, starting with an overview of what the app does and a getting started guide that takes you to your first result.

Make your first steps with the following features:

## Triply Academy

The [Academy](../../triplydb-changelog/index.md) explains the ideas the Triply products are built on: linked data,
knowledge graphs, ontologies, and the standards for querying and validating them.

It is deliberately product-neutral. You do not need any of it to use a Triply
product, and none of it tells you what to click (the product documentation does
that). Read the Academy when you want to understand *why* things work the way they
do, or when a term in the product documentation turns out to have more behind it
than a definition.
